## Projects

**<a href="https://prydz.dreemachine.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prydz Tracklister</a>**  
A stats tracker & generator for Eric Prydz fans. Users can pick every show they've attended and see their most-heard tracks, rarest catches, and a map of live listening history. I built it end to end: data scraping/cleaning pipeline, geocoding, and a client-side site with no backend or login.
