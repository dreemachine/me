How to use this map

×
Dots
Purple = student (current location)
Colored = caretaker origin
Lines Lines connect students to their caretakers' origins. Color indicates the origin country (see legend).
Rings around a student dot A ring means one or more caretakers were born in the same city as the student. Each ring = one caretaker.
×2 badge Appears on a line when you select a student and both caretakers share the same origin city.
Interactions
🔵 Click a student in the sidebar to highlight their connections.
🟢 Click an origin in the legend to highlight all lines from that country — dash patterns distinguish students.
📍 Click a caretaker dot on the map to highlight lines and students connected to that location.
